# RH Bridge Pro

Advanced and user-friendly bridging tool for moving assets and expanding tokens from Base to Robinhood Chain using LayerZero OFT standard.

## Commands

- "bridge 1000 USDC from base to robinhood"
- "bridge my token to robinhood chain with liquidity"
- "expand my memecoin to robinhood"
- "check my bridge status"
- "bridge with auto liquidity seeding"
- "bridge 5000 USDC and add liquidity"

## Capabilities

- LayerZero OFT (Omnichain Fungible Token) bridging
- Automatic liquidity pool seeding on Robinhood Chain
- Token expansion / deployment support
- Real-time bridge transaction tracking
- Gas optimization & cost estimation
- Smart error handling and retry logic
- Support for native tokens and major stablecoins

## Example Usage

```text
Bridge 2500 USDC from Base to Robinhood Chain with automatic liquidity seeding

Expand my memecoin to Robinhood Chain with 8000 WETH initial liquidity

Check the status of my last bridge transaction
```

## Supported Chains

- Base → Robinhood Chain (primary)
- Robinhood Chain → Base (in development)

## Notes

- Always review the transaction details in Bankr before confirming
- Liquidity seeding helps your token get immediate trading volume on Robinhood Chain
- Recommended for users who want to launch or expand tokens to Robinhood Chain
