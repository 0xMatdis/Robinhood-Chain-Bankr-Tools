# RH Yield Optimizer

Intelligent yield farming and optimization skill that maximizes returns by using tokenized stocks (NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, etc.) as collateral.

## Commands

- "optimize my yield on robinhood"
- "find best yield for my NVDA holdings"
- "lend my tokenized stocks for maximum yield"
- "show highest APY opportunities right now"
- "auto compound my yield every week"
- "analyze yield vs risk for my portfolio"

## Capabilities

- Real-time yield opportunity scanning across protocols
- Risk-adjusted yield recommendations
- Auto-compounding strategies
- Collateral management & health monitoring
- Portfolio yield tracking and reporting
- Smart allocation between different yield pools
- Gas-efficient execution

## Example Usage

```text
Optimize yield for my 15 NVDA on Robinhood Chain

Find the highest risk-adjusted APY using TSLA as collateral

Show me all yield opportunities above 12% APY right now

Auto-compound my yield every 7 days on the best available pool
```

## Supported Assets

NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, SPY, QQQ, AMD, META, GOOGL, and other major tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain.

## Notes

- Using tokenized stocks as collateral can generate high yield but carries liquidation risk
- Always monitor collateral health when using leverage
- Best combined with `rh-portfolio-manager` for balanced yield + risk strategy
