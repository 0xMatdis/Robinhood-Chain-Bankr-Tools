# RH Yield Optimizer

Smart yield farming & optimization tool using tokenized stocks (NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, etc) as collateral.

## Commands

- "optimize my yield on robinhood"
- "find best yield for my NVDA holdings"
- "lend my tokenized stocks for yield"
- "show highest APY opportunities on robinhood"
- "auto compound my yield every week"

## Capabilities

- Find highest yield opportunities using stock tokens as collateral
- Lending / Borrowing strategies
- Auto-compounding
- Risk-adjusted yield recommendations
- Cross-protocol yield aggregation
- Portfolio yield tracking

## Example Usage

"Optimize yield for my 10 NVDA on Robinhood Chain"
"Find safest high yield strategy with TSLA collateral"
"Show me all yield opportunities above 15% APY right now"

## Supported Protocols
- Lending pools on Robinhood Chain
- Yield vaults & structured products
- Collateralized borrowing
