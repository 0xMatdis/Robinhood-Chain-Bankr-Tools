# RH Portfolio Manager

Advanced AI-powered portfolio management skill designed specifically for tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain. Handles rebalancing, DCA scheduling, performance tracking, and risk optimization.

## Commands

- "rebalance my portfolio on robinhood"
- "analyze my tokenized stocks portfolio"
- "set up weekly DCA every Monday"
- "show my portfolio performance and risk level"
- "create a balanced portfolio with 1000 USDC"
- "reduce risk in my current holdings"
- "rebalance to 40% NVDA, 30% AAPL, 20% TSLA"

## Capabilities

- Smart automated rebalancing based on performance and risk
- Dollar Cost Averaging (DCA) with custom schedules
- Portfolio PnL tracking and analytics
- Risk assessment & correlation analysis between stocks
- Intelligent allocation suggestions
- Gas-efficient batch execution via Bankr
- Support for major tokenized stocks

## Example Usage

```text
Rebalance my portfolio to 40% NVDA, 30% AAPL, 20% TSLA, 10% SPY on Robinhood Chain

Set up weekly DCA of 150 USDC every Monday into the top 3 performing stocks

Analyze my current portfolio performance and suggest risk adjustments
```

## Supported Assets

NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, SPY, QQQ, AMD, META, GOOGL, and other major tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain.

## Notes

- Best used for long-term or medium-term portfolio management
- Always review the proposed transactions in Bankr before confirming
- Can be combined with other skills (scanner, yield optimizer, etc.)
