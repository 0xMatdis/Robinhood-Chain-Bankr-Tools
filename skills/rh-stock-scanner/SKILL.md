# RH Stock Scanner

Real-time scanner untuk menemukan peluang trading tokenized stocks di Robinhood Chain.

## Commands

- "scan momentum stocks on robinhood"
- "show top gaining tokenized stocks"
- "find high volume stocks on robinhood"
- "scan correlation between stocks and crypto"
- "show earnings plays this week"

## Capabilities

- Real-time momentum & volume scanner
- Top gainers / losers detection
- Correlation analysis (stock vs crypto)
- Earnings calendar integration
- Smart opportunity scoring
- Filter berdasarkan market cap, volume, dan volatility

## Example Usage

"Scan strongest tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain right now"
"Find stocks with volume spike over 300% today"
"Show me momentum plays with good correlation to BTC"

## Supported Assets
NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, SPY, QQQ, AMD, META, GOOGL, dll.
