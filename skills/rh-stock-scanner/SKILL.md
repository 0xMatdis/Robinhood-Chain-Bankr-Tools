# RH Stock Scanner

Powerful real-time market scanner designed to help AI agents and users discover high-potential trading opportunities on tokenized stocks in Robinhood Chain.

## Commands

- "scan momentum stocks on robinhood"
- "show top gaining tokenized stocks"
- "find high volume stocks today"
- "scan correlation between stocks and crypto"
- "show best opportunities right now"
- "scan for earnings plays this week"

## Capabilities

- Real-time momentum and price action detection
- Volume spike analysis
- Top gainers and losers ranking
- Correlation analysis (stocks vs BTC/ETH)
- Smart opportunity scoring system
- Earnings calendar integration
- Custom filters (market cap, volume, volatility, etc.)
- Rapid market overview

## Example Usage

```text
Scan for the strongest momentum tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain right now

Show me top 5 stocks with volume increase over 200% in the last 24 hours

Find tokenized stocks with high correlation to BTC that are currently undervalued

Scan for the best earnings plays this week
```

## Supported Assets

NVDA, AAPL, TSLA, SPY, QQQ, AMD, META, GOOGL, and other major tokenized stocks on Robinhood Chain.

## Notes

- Scanner runs in real-time using on-chain and market data
- Higher volume and momentum usually indicate stronger opportunities
- Can be combined with `rh-portfolio-manager` or `rh-earnings-agent` for powerful strategies
